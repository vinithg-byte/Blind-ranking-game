HOW TO PLAY

One reveal. One decision.

  1. Choose a Tamil cinema category and challenge.
  2. One mystery item is revealed at a time.
  3. Place it into one of the ten ranking slots.
  4. That position locks. No moving it later.
  5. Rank all ten items, then reveal your final list.
